Scenario: `inventory-alchemy`. Fixture: Original 12-item inventory, three recipes and capacity limit.

Reset ownership: Restore item counts and transaction/request ledger.

Limits and unavailable route: Project-owned game economy example; no purchase or payment path.

Use original bounded fixtures with hash/license/seed manifests. Readiness must name the actual missing adapter and a useful labeled fallback if possible. Fixture replay does not establish device/provider support.

### Positive acceptance

- Recipe consumes/produces exact quantities
- Duplicate request crafts once
- Capacity failure leaves inventory unchanged
- Undo restores prior counts

### Failure and negative controls

- Unknown ingredient rejects
- Stale proposal refuses commit

Additionally exercise reset/exit during the longest operation, repeated entry and fixture isolation. Observe real mechanisms; never assign the expected final result to make the assertion pass.
