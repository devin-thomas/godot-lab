### Positive acceptance

- One swing damages target once
- Hitstop pauses intended actors while UI remains active
- Cancel accepted only in window
- Invulnerability suppresses declared hits

### Failure and negative controls

- Unknown attack rejects
- Repeated overlap cannot double damage same swing

Additionally exercise reset/exit during the longest operation, repeated entry and fixture isolation. Observe real mechanisms; never assign the expected final result to make the assertion pass.
