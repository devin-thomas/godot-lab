# LAB-001: Motion atelier

Scenario: `motion`. State: automated-verified on the recorded Windows build; human/device gates deferred. See BUILD_STATUS for evidence.

The player moves and jumps around solid stairs and a coral obstacle. Floor, walls, and the player's collision shape give an immediate demonstration of CharacterBody3D behavior. A lab seal records meaningful movement plus a jump; it does not represent reaching a separate goal trigger.

## Play and reset

Move with the host controls and jump. Watch the character-ground relationship and the visible movement state. The lab explanation identifies collisions, gravity, and movement. Reset restores the start transform and clears transient motion. Exit returns to normal hub navigation.

## Acceptance

The real character must contact the floor, remain blocked by the solid coral obstacle, and rise/fall during jump. The scenario records traveled distance, jump count, and landing. Repeat reset and re-entry. An automated driver must move the actual character; assigning its final expected location is insufficient.

Headless checks establish collision/motion outcomes. Rendered footage supports inspection of the player and obstacles. Human feel and physical controller behavior remain deferred. Future 2D movement is a separate catalog experiment rather than a claim of this 3D lab.
