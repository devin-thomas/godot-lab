### Fixture, reset and unavailable path

Scenario: `golden-assertions`. Fixture: Original calibrated frames, PCM tone and semantic state records.

Reset ownership: Discard unapproved reference proposals and restore comparison settings.

Limits and unavailable route: Tolerances avoid brittle exact pixels; passing metrics do not establish overall artistic quality.

Use original bounded fixtures with hash/license/seed manifests. Readiness must name the actual missing adapter and a useful labeled fallback if possible. Fixture replay does not establish device/provider support.
