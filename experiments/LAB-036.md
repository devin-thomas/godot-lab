### Fixture, reset and unavailable path

Scenario: `profiling-booth`. Fixture: Original draw-heavy and script-heavy fixtures with fixed camera.

Reset ownership: Restore workload, camera and profiler sampling state.

Limits and unavailable route: No universal performance threshold or hardware claim without measurements.

Use original bounded fixtures with hash/license/seed manifests. Readiness must name the actual missing adapter and a useful labeled fallback if possible. Fixture replay does not establish device/provider support.

### Positive acceptance

- Report identifies warm-up/sample duration
- Unavailable counters stay missing
- Optimization preserves declared visible outcome
- Samples include variance not only best frame

### Failure and negative controls

- Sample budget too small rejects
- Concurrent workload warns or invalidates comparison

Additionally exercise reset/exit during the longest operation, repeated entry and fixture isolation. Observe real mechanisms; never assign the expected final result to make the assertion pass.
