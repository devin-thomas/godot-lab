# LAB-005: Signal chamber

Scenario: `audio`. State: automated-verified on the recorded Windows build, including encoded spatial audio; human/device gates deferred. See BUILD_STATUS for evidence.

Move around a visible emitter and observe the spatial relationship between source and listener. Original synthetic tones provide a rights-safe fixture. Visible state keeps the experiment understandable even on a muted machine.

## Play and reset

The operation starts or changes the emitter's audible example; movement changes its relation to the listener. Show source position and playback state. Reset stops/restarts the baseline deliberately, and exit stops the lab's audio.

## Acceptance

Assert emitter placement and playback lifecycle, then inspect a non-headless encoded audio artifact. Measure non-silent energy, duration, and stereo differences where panning is claimed. A headless dummy audio driver or `playing=true` cannot establish audible output. Check that reset/exit leaves no unwanted looping source.

Encoded audio analysis does not prove real speakers/headphones, hearing comfort, or physical-device audio routing. Those gates remain deferred.

Reference: [AudioStreamPlayer3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_audiostreamplayer3d.html).
