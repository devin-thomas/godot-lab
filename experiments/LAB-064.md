### Interaction

1. Place an original gradient card
2. Move source/camera
3. Compare against dynamic shadow reference
4. Inspect overlap and reset

### Fixture, reset and unavailable path

Scenario: `shadow-cards`. Fixture: Original contact gradient and intersecting shaft textures.

Reset ownership: Remove placed cards and restore fixed comparison rig.

Limits and unavailable route: Cards are not volumetric lighting or physically correct moving shadows.

Use original bounded fixtures with hash/license/seed manifests. Readiness must name the actual missing adapter and a useful labeled fallback if possible. Fixture replay does not establish device/provider support.

### Positive acceptance

- Card affects intended recess
- Unsupported angle artifacts are disclosed
- Alpha overlap stays bounded
- Reset removes all added cards

### Failure and negative controls

- Invalid texture rights/source rejects
- Strength outside range refuses

Additionally exercise reset/exit during the longest operation, repeated entry and fixture isolation. Observe real mechanisms; never assign the expected final result to make the assertion pass.
