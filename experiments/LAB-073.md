### Interaction

1. Select original recorded fixture
2. Play with captions
3. Seek to a cue
4. Compare encoded timing and transcript provenance

### Fixture, reset and unavailable path

Scenario: `voice-captions`. Fixture: Original consented short speech plus authoritative cue text/times.

Reset ownership: Stop voice and clear active cue/time offset.

Limits and unavailable route: ASR is draft evidence; reviewed authoritative captions are required. No microphone permission assumed.

Use original bounded fixtures with hash/license/seed manifests. Readiness must name the actual missing adapter and a useful labeled fallback if possible. Fixture replay does not establish device/provider support.

### Positive acceptance

- Cue appears within timing tolerance
- Seek selects correct cue
- Muted route retains captions
- Transcript/corrections preserve exact fixture wording

### Failure and negative controls

- Malformed overlapping policy violation rejects track
- Missing audio gives labeled text-only fallback

Additionally exercise reset/exit during the longest operation, repeated entry and fixture isolation. Observe real mechanisms; never assign the expected final result to make the assertion pass.
