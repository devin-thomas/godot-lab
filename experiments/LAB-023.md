### Interaction

1. Choose a seed and room budget
2. Generate a preview
3. Inspect connected route
4. Commit and walk the result

### Fixture, reset and unavailable path

Scenario: `procedural-garden`. Fixture: Original grid modules, entrances and obstacle tiles.

Reset ownership: Restore fixture seed and discard uncommitted proposals.

Limits and unavailable route: Procedural recipe is lab-owned; no claim of universal level quality.

Use original bounded fixtures with hash/license/seed manifests. Readiness must name the actual missing adapter and a useful labeled fallback if possible. Fixture replay does not establish device/provider support.

### Positive acceptance

- Same seed/config yields same topology hash
- Every required room connects
- Budget caps hold
- Failed proposal leaves current world unchanged

### Failure and negative controls

- Impossible connectivity rejects proposal
- Cancel prevents later completion from committing

Additionally exercise reset/exit during the longest operation, repeated entry and fixture isolation. Observe real mechanisms; never assign the expected final result to make the assertion pass.
