# LAB-002: Gravity foundry

Scenario: `physics`. State: automated-verified on the recorded Windows build; human/device gates deferred. See BUILD_STATUS for evidence.

Launch a real rigid body into a resettable arrangement. Its travel, contacts, and settling reveal the difference between a direct character controller and a simulated body.

## Play and reset

The lab operation applies an impulse to its source body. Let the result unfold, inspect its displacement and contacts, then reset to the baseline arrangement. Repeated operation is bounded rather than spawning an unlimited body stream. Exit releases the arrangement.

## Acceptance

Detect meaningful post-impulse displacement, collision response, and settling within a documented timeout/tolerance. Reset restores transforms and clears velocity. Repeated entry/action/reset must not leak bodies or stack residual impulses. Scenario replay compares semantic events and meaningful outcomes; physics is not promised to be bit-identical across machines.

Rendered evidence must show the arrangement and reaction. Headless displacement alone cannot establish useful camera framing or convincing feedback.

Reference: [RigidBody3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_rigidbody3d.html).
