Limits and unavailable route: Preview checks do not prove comfort. Avoid compulsory rapid flashing.

### Positive acceptance

- Reduced motion suppresses declared effects
- No-flash keeps steady focus
- Text-scale retains essential actions
- Preferences survive restart

### Failure and negative controls

- Invalid scale/intensity rejects
- Cancelled reset preserves chosen preferences

Additionally exercise reset/exit during the longest operation, repeated entry and fixture isolation. Observe real mechanisms; never assign the expected final result to make the assertion pass.
